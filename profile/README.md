GateRook is a desktop gateway in development for connecting databases, logs, APIs, and cloud services to AI assistants through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Its design centers on three principles:

- **Useful context.** Connect the services your AI needs to investigate and understand your stack.
- **Deliberate changes.** Review the exact proposed action and its target before it runs.
- **Local control.** Manage connections, credentials, and access rules through your own gateway.

The goal is less time copying logs and query results, with clear boundaries for what AI can access and change.

**In development:** the public repository is being prepared for application sources. Installers are not yet published.
